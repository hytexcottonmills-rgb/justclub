# JustClub Local SEO Content Factual Verification Audit

**Audit Date:** September 22, 2026  
**Auditor:** JustClub Quality & Engineering Assurance  
**Target:** Localized city content in `seo/data/cities.ts` and generated static pages (`/locations/india/*/`)  
**Scope:** Factual accuracy, defensibility, and compliance with the Data Integrity Rule across all 12 city pages.

---

## Executive Summary

A comprehensive factual verification audit was conducted on all 12 localized city pages in the JustClub SEO architecture. The audit evaluated every factual claim, statistical assertion, operating hour generalization, cloth brand specification, and local market statement against the three-tier classification framework:

- **Category A (Verified Local Fact):** Retained directly (real municipal geography, state boundaries, established commercial/recreational neighborhoods, coastal humidity characteristics).
- **Category B (General Operational Guidance):** Rewritten into clear operational guidance for venue operators rather than empirical survey assertions about what all local clubs do.
- **Category C (Unsupported Local Generalization):** Removed or reframed (e.g., claiming specific cloth brand preferences for individual cities, claiming universal 24/7 AC operation, claiming round-the-clock shift operation in specific suburbs, or claiming identical multi-category venue types across all micro-neighborhoods).

No URLs were changed. No new pages were created. The 64-page sitemap architecture remains strictly intact.
