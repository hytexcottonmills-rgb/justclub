---

## 1. Qualitative Scoring Methodology

Every topic and URL is evaluated across five pillars:
1. **Relevance (1-5)**: How central is the topic to JustClub's core operating features (table timers, billing, khata, UPI)?
2. **Commercial Intent (1-5)**: Is the searcher actively looking to buy/implement software or solve an immediate financial problem?
3. **Product Fit (1-5)**: Does JustClub deliver a superior product experience compared to generic alternatives?
4. **Search Intent Strength (1-5)**: How specific and well-defined is the search query?
5. **Competitive Opportunity (1-5)**: Can JustClub easily outrank competitors (like CueFlow or generic POS) due to deeper local/functional depth?

### Priority Tier Classification:
- **P0 (Critical Optimization of Existing High-Value Pages)**: Core commercial engines and top converting landing pages. Immediate focus.
- **P1 (High-Value Content & Feature Gaps)**: Deepening high-intent pages with rich schema, local context, and interactive bridges.
- **P2 (Supporting Authority Content)**: Operational manuals, tournament guides, and maintenance SOPs that build topical authority.
- **P3 (Experimental / Long-Tail Exploration)**: Minor local hubs and niche cue sports queries.
